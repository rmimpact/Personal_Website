Lab Support App is an operational tool for invited University of Technology Sydney Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology lab-support personnel.

Lab Support App is operated by Remy Moscovitz. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “the operator” refer to Remy Moscovitz.

Privacy contact: mail@remymoscovitz.com

This policy explains what information Lab Support App handles, why it is used, who may receive it, and how users can request access, correction or deletion. It covers the app and its supporting services. It does not replace the separate privacy notices of UTS or third-party service providers.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, Lab Support App handles the following information.

Identity and sign-in information

  • The name you enter when using shared team access.
  • Your account identifier, name, email address and any available profile image when you use Microsoft sign-in.
  • Account approval and administrator status.
  • Authentication credentials and session tokens needed to maintain access.

Operational information

  • Building, room, printer, cabinet, key and equipment information.
  • Checks, stock counts, notes, incidents and exam-support schedules.
  • Staff names, account references and timestamps associated with submitted work.

Photos and screenshots

  • Images you choose to upload for buildings, cabinets, keys, keysets or other supported records.
  • Images you explicitly choose to send for AI-assisted stock counting or exam-schedule extraction.

Optional Jamf information

  • A server address and API-client credentials or account credentials you enter.
  • Managed-device information retrieved from the configured organisation’s Jamf Pro service.
  • Recent-device references stored locally.
  • Device security information retrieved on demand where your Jamf permissions allow it.

Technical information

Our hosting, authentication, AI and linked-service providers may process IP addresses, request information, authentication events, device or browser characteristics, errors and security logs when providing their services.

2. How information is collected

Information is collected when you enter it, submit operational changes, upload an image, sign in, configure an integration or use a connected service.

Operational information may also have been entered by another authorised team member or supplied through an organisation’s connected systems.

The app does not continuously monitor your camera or photo library. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone or precise device location.

Selecting a photo gives the app access to the image you choose. Camera access is requested when you choose a camera feature.

3. Why information is used

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users and manage access.
  • Identify who completed operational work.
  • Coordinate lab checks, equipment support and related activities.
  • Store and display records and selected images.
  • Provide optional image extraction and managed-device lookup features.
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.
  • Diagnose faults, protect the service and investigate misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Lab Support App does not sell personal information, display advertising or use personal information for advertising profiles. The native app does not include advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

4. Shared team access and Microsoft accounts

Shared team access

Shared-code users access the app through a shared technical account and enter a name to identify their work.

The entered name is not a separate personal authentication account. It is stored locally and may be included in operational records visible to authorised team members.

Signing out removes the local crew name and Lab Support App session. It does not remove names or records already submitted to the shared service.

To request correction or deletion of your personal information in those records, contact mail@remymoscovitz.com.

Microsoft sign-in

Microsoft sign-in creates an individual Lab Support App authentication record and profile. Access may require administrator approval.

You can initiate deletion through:

Settings → Privacy & Data → Request Account Deletion

Users awaiting approval can reach these settings through Privacy & Account.

Deletion requests are processed manually, with a target completion time of 30 days. We will confirm completion and explain any information that must be retained by law.

Deleting a Lab Support App account does not delete your Microsoft account or UTS account.

5. Who information is shared with

Authorised team members and administrators

Operational records, submitted images and staff attribution may be visible to other authorised users so they can coordinate their work.

Supabase

Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, image storage and server-side functions.

Microsoft

Microsoft handles optional institutional sign-in and supplies the identity information needed to create and maintain a Lab Support App profile.

Google Gemini

When you explicitly confirm an AI action, the selected image is sent through a server-side service to Google’s Gemini API for stock counting or exam-schedule extraction. Section 6 explains this processing.

Jamf Pro

When configured, the app communicates directly with the organisation’s Jamf Pro server using credentials you supply. Requests and access may be logged by that organisation’s Jamf service.

Jamf credentials are not sent to Supabase or Google Gemini as part of the Jamf integration.

Labs Logger and linked services

Opening embedded or external services sends ordinary web requests to those services. Their operators may receive technical information, including an IP address and browser details, under their own privacy practices.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents or misuse.

6. Optional AI image processing

AI features are optional. You can enter stock quantities or exam details manually instead.

Before sending an image, the app identifies Google Gemini as the recipient and asks you to confirm. Selecting an image alone does not authorise its transmission to Google.

The selected image, including any visible text and background content, is transmitted for processing. Instructions to extract only relevant fields do not remove other information from the image before transmission.

Do not submit personal information, confidential material, passwords, identification documents or unrelated sensitive content. Crop or redact images before sending them.

Google’s handling depends on the applicable API service plan and settings. Under unpaid-service terms, submissions and generated responses may be used to improve Google products, and human reviewers may review them. Paid-service terms provide different restrictions on product-improvement use. Security and abuse-monitoring processing may still apply.

See the Gemini API terms ↗.

The app does not save an AI input image as an operational photo merely because it was analysed. Separately choosing to save that image as a record photo uploads it to image storage.

AI results may be inaccurate. Review and correct extracted information before saving it.

7. Uploaded photos and image links

Images you choose to save are uploaded to hosted storage.

The current app uses public image URLs for uploaded photos. A person with a valid image URL may be able to view that image without signing in to Lab Support App.

Do not upload personal information, confidential documents or security-sensitive material as record photos.

Removing or replacing an image displayed in a record does not necessarily erase the original stored file. Contact mail@remymoscovitz.com to request deletion of a stored image.

8. Device storage and security

On iOS, Lab Support App authentication tokens and saved Jamf credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Temporary Jamf access tokens are held in memory while needed.

Preferences, the entered crew name and recent-device references may be stored locally.

Signing out of Lab Support App does not remove configured Jamf servers or their credentials. Remove saved Jamf servers through Jamf Server Settings when you no longer want them stored.

Keychain items can survive uninstalling an app. Do not rely on uninstalling alone to erase saved credentials.

The app uses HTTPS connections and database access controls. These protections do not make the public image links described above private. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Users should protect their device, team access code and integration credentials, and only configure services they are authorised to access.

9. Storage locations and international processing

Information may be processed outside Australia.

The current Supabase primary database is hosted in Mumbai, India. Other providers and their subprocessors may process information in additional countries according to their service infrastructure and agreements.

Contact us if you need more information about a particular provider or processing arrangement.

10. Retention and deletion

Operational records are retained while needed to run the service and maintain relevant work history. There is not currently one automatic deletion period covering every operational record.

Personal information should be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Account deletion includes associated personal information and user-created content, except information that must be retained by law. Operational records may remain where they no longer identify the person or retention is legally required.

Provider logs and any backups follow the applicable provider’s retention arrangements. Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove information from retained backups or security logs.

Local preferences and recent-device references remain until removed or the relevant local app data is deleted. Saved credentials are handled as described in Section 8.

11. Your choices and requests

You can:

  • Decline optional camera access.
  • Choose which images to share through the system photo picker.
  • Decline AI processing and enter information manually.
  • Remove configured Jamf servers.
  • Sign out of Lab Support App.
  • Request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held through the app.
  • Initiate deletion of an individual Microsoft-linked Lab Support App account from within the app.

To make a privacy request, email mail@remymoscovitz.com with the subject Lab Support App privacy.

Describe the information or record concerned. Do not send passwords, access codes, recovery codes or API secrets. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing personal information.

Withdrawing permission for future optional processing does not automatically erase information already submitted. You can separately request deletion.

Where information must be retained by law, we will explain the reason and the information affected.

12. Children and authorised use

Lab Support App is intended for invited workplace personnel and is not directed to children.

Optional Gemini-powered features are intended for users aged 18 or older, consistent with the provider’s service requirements.

Downloading the app does not itself authorise access to institutional systems or operational information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, service providers or information-handling practices change.

The updated policy will show a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.
